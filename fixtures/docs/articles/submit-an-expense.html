Submit an expense

Record an out-of-pocket expense so it can be reviewed and reimbursed in the next payroll run.

Before you start. Any employee can submit an expense.

Steps

  1. In the left sidebar, click Expenses.
  2. Click New expense.
  3. Enter the Merchant and the Amount.
  4. Choose a Category - Travel, Meals or Software.
  5. Click Save expense.

What happens next

The expense appears at the top of the list with the status Pending.
